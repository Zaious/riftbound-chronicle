### Kha'Zix - Voidreaver (Body+Chaos)

Legend ability: `When you win a combat, gain 1 XP. Spend 1 XP, Exhaust: Buff a unit. Spend 2 XP, Exhaust: Move an exhausted friendly unit from a battlefield to its base.` **Kha'Zix - Evolving Hunter** (Body): `Hunt. When I conquer or hold, gain 1 XP. When I attack, you may spend 3 XP to deal damage equal to my Might to an enemy unit here.` **Kha'Zix - Mutating Horror** (Chaos): `Ambush. When I attack or defend, if an enemy unit is alone here, give me +2 might this turn and gain 2 XP.`

**Archetype**: Control/attrition. The Legend's trigger — win a combat, gain XP — is reactive to combat resolution and feeds a resource pool spent on utility (buff a unit, or pull an exhausted unit to safety), not on a burst payoff. It rewards repeatedly winning fights and reinvesting into keeping the same units strong and alive across many turns, rather than either racing early or holding for one detonation. Evolving Hunter reinforces this directly: Hunt grants XP from holding, not just winning, tying the resource to sustained territory control, and its 3-XP spend is a big attack-triggered removal option that clears the way to keep conquering. Mutating Horror pulls slightly toward tempo (an immediate +2 Might against isolated targets), but its smaller, situational XP gain reads as an aggressive branch of the same grind engine, not a separate lane.

**Core unfolding**: Early game wants winnable fights — favorable Showdowns generally, or isolated 1v1s for Mutating Horror — to start the XP economy. Once running, XP funds either ongoing stat buffs that keep already-committed units ahead turn after turn, or Evolving Hunter's expensive attack-triggered removal that clears blockers to keep conquering; the 2-XP recall-to-base spend explicitly protects a threatened unit from a bad trade so it can fight again later, an attrition tool through and through.

**Engine to points**: This is a win-then-hold plan — Hunt rewards holding a battlefield directly with the same resource combat wins generate, and every XP spend exists to keep winning subsequent fights at that or a new battlefield. It converts combat success into sustained board control, not a single point swing or independent card generation.
