### Rumble - Mechanized Menace (Fury+Mind)

Legend ability: `Your Mechs have Shield. (+1 might while they're defenders.)` **Rumble - Hotheaded** (Fury): `Your Mechs each have Assault. When I conquer, you may recycle another friendly unit to play a Mech from your trash. Reduce its Energy cost by the Might of the unit you recycled.` **Rumble - Scrapper** (Mind): `Your Mechs have +1 might (including me). When I hold, play a 3 might Mech unit token to your base.`

Archetype: hybrid, and the split runs right through the two Champions. The Legend's own ability is static and defensive by default — Shield only boosts Mechs while defending, tone-setting a tribe that's naturally good at holding ground. Hotheaded's trigger is on-conquer: it rewards actively capturing battlefields by recycling a unit to discount a Mech pulled back from the trash, a snowballing recursion engine tied to offense (it also grants Assault, an attacking keyword). Scrapper's trigger is on-hold: it rewards sustained, uncontested control of a battlefield by generating a free 3-might Mech token every time that condition is met, purely a defensive-accumulation engine.

Core unfolding: with Hotheaded, the deck wants to conquer battlefields, feeding spent units back into cheaper recurred Mechs and pushing forward again — an aggressive, self-refueling loop built on repeated capture. With Scrapper, the deck instead wants to plant Mechs and hold, letting Shield-boosted defenders sit still while the hold-trigger generates free reinforcement tokens turn after turn, growing the board without spending cards. Both readings share the same chassis: buffed, Shielded Mechs going wide.

Win condition: Scrapper's line is explicitly hold-to-generate — the longer a battlefield stays held, the more free bodies (defensively strengthened by Shield) arrive, making that position progressively harder to dislodge. Hotheaded's line is repeated-conquest snowballing — each capture is reinvested into more attackers via trash recursion. The Legend doesn't commit to one lane; the Champion pick does.
