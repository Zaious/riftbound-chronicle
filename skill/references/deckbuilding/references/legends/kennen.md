### Kennen, Heart of the Tempest (Order+Chaos)

> Data note: the source packet for this Legend paired it with several champion cards that turned out to be unrelated Legends' cards (a data artifact, not real Kennen options) and had to be discarded. Only the two entries actually matching Kennen's Order+Chaos domains are used below — treat this entry as lower-confidence than the rest of this file until the underlying source data is cleaned up and re-verified.

Legend ability: `When you play a card from anywhere other than your hand, empower me. Action: Disempower me, Exhaust: Give a unit Assault 2 this turn.` **Kennen, Storm of Shuriken** (Chaos): `When you play me, Burn 2. When I conquer, give a spell in your trash Flow equal to its cost this turn.` **Kennen, Keeper of Balance** (Order): `Hidden. When you play me or I attack, you may pay 2 Energy to Stun a unit. While there's a stunned enemy unit here, I have +2 might.`

The Legend trigger is conditional on playing cards from non-hand zones — trash, Hidden, or via Flow — and its payoff is banked as a stackable "Empowered" state, spent later for a combat buff. That's a two-phase structure: accumulate charges from off-hand plays, then cash them in right before an attack. Storm of Shuriken builds the accumulation half directly — Burn 2 seeds the trash with cards on play, and its conquer trigger grants Flow to a trashed spell, letting you recast it from trash (itself a non-hand play that re-triggers empower) — a self-feeding loop. Keeper of Balance contributes from the Hidden side (resolving from Hidden is itself a non-hand play) plus an attack-triggered Stun that clears a blocker and grows Kennen's own Might while a Stunned enemy is present. Both feed the same Empowered engine, but the payoff — Assault 2 on an attacker — is a combat-swing tool, not a resource or holding tool.

Core unfolding, held loosely given the data caveat above: early turns want Storm of Shuriken on board to start milling the trash and generate the first conquer-triggered Flow recast, which both refuels the trash-recursion loop and stacks another Empowered charge — a patient buildup phase. Keeper of Balance's Hidden line can run in parallel, adding a second non-hand-play source and a Stun tool to keep blockers off the board while charges accumulate. The closing turn spends banked Empowered charges via repeated activations to hand out Assault 2 to one or more attackers right before a combat phase, pushing several Showdowns at once. It converts into points by winning Showdowns outright through stacked combat buffs — a combo-flavored engine (patient non-hand-play accumulation, then a turn that swings multiple attacks at once) rather than a pure grind-and-hold control plan.
