### Vex, Gloomist (Calm+Chaos)

Legend ability: `When you or an ally hold, you may exhaust me to draw 1.` **Vex, Mocking** (Calm): `Shield. Tank. When you Stun an enemy unit at a battlefield, you may move me to that battlefield.` **Vex, Cheerless** (Chaos): `While I'm in combat, friendly spells cost 1 Energy and a rainbow rune less to a minimum of 1 Energy, and enemy spells cost 1 Energy and a rainbow rune more.`

The Legend ability's trigger is "hold" — the literal scoring action of the win condition — converted directly into card draw. That's an unambiguous control signal: the ability doesn't reward attacking or conquering, it rewards the specific act of sitting on a Battlefield you already have, refueling your hand precisely for having done so. Mocking reinforces this with stats built to survive combat as a defender (Shield, Tank) plus a reactive relocation trigger off Stunning an enemy — repositioning toward contested ground after neutralizing a threat, not before. Cheerless is more of a Showdown-combat tax (cheapening your spells, taxing the opponent's) that can serve either lane, but it still operates inside combat rather than racing to end it early.

Core unfolding: get a Vex body established on a Battlefield early enough to start holding, then let the Legend ability's draw trigger fire every turn that hold persists — this is a slow-building card-advantage engine that only works once you already have board position, so the early game is about securing that first foothold cheaply rather than overextending. Once running, Mocking's Stun-triggered movement lets you consolidate onto whichever Battlefield you've just cleared, and Cheerless's combat tax makes each individual Showdown easier to win with fewer resources. The engine converts into points by holding uncontested over multiple turns, with the draw trigger supplying the answers needed to keep denying the opponent's attempts to dislodge you.
