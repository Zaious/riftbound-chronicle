### Zed, Master of Shadows (Fury+Chaos)

Legend ability: `When you banish a card you own, empower me. Action: Disempower me, Exhaust: Discard 1, then draw 1.` **Zed, From the Shadows** (Fury): `You may discard 1 as an additional cost to play me. When you play me, if you paid the additional cost, play a 0 might Shadow Clone unit token. ("When I attack, you may banish a unit from your trash. If you do, give me Assault 4 this turn.")` **Zed, Without a Sound** (Chaos): `When I conquer, play a 0 might Shadow Clone unit token to your base. Action: 1 Energy and a Chaos rune: Move me and a Shadow Clone you control to each other's locations.`

The Legend ability rewards banishing your own cards with a loot effect (discard, then draw) — a consistency tool that keeps the hand fresh, not a combat payoff by itself. What drives the deck is what feeds banish triggers. From the Shadows commits hard to explosive aggro: discarding to play Zed stocks the trash, spawns a disposable Shadow Clone, and that Clone's attack trigger can banish a trash unit for a massive Assault 4 swing — which also empowers the Legend for a follow-up loot. This needs the trash stocked before the swing turn, so early turns must feed the graveyard. Without a Sound is comparatively grindier: conquering mints another Clone into your base (compounding attacker count turn over turn) and the swap ability repositions Zed and a Clone between locations — useful either to pull Zed to safety or to slot a fresh attacker into an active fight. Both Champions still require conquering or attacking to generate value, so neither pulls toward control; the split is between one explosive burst-combat build (From the Shadows) and one sustained multi-turn pressure build (Without a Sound) — a hybrid aggro/combo texture rather than a clean single lane.

Core unfolding: with From the Shadows, the early game discards cards to play Zed and stock the trash, a Shadow Clone token appears immediately as a disposable body; once it attacks, banishing a trash unit turns it into a one-shot Assault 4 threat capable of blowing through most blockers, and that same banish empowers the Legend for a loot activation to refill the hand for the next cycle. With Without a Sound, each successful conquer leaves behind another 0-might Clone, so board presence compounds the more you win, and the swap ability lets you keep threatening a fresh location without fully committing Zed himself to risk. Either way the engine converts into points by winning Showdowns outright — the Legend's own loot ability just sustains the hand so the aggression doesn't run out of gas.
