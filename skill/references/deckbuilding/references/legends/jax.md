### Jax - Grandmaster At Arms (Calm+Body)

Legend ability: `1 Energy, Exhaust: Attach a detached Equipment you control to a unit you control. Exhaust: Attach an attached Equipment you control to a unit you control.` Champions: **Jax - Unmatched** (Calm) — `Deflect. Each Equipment in your hand has Quick-Draw. (It gains Reaction. When you play it, attach it to a unit you control.)` **Jax - Unrelenting** (Body) — `Weaponmaster. When you attach an Equipment to me, you may pay 1 Energy to draw 1.`

The Legend ability is a plain repeatable activated ability — no opponent-dependent condition, no burst window — that redeploys Equipment between units at will. That's a flexibility/resilience tool rather than a race or setup-payoff mechanic: whichever unit currently needs the gear (about to fight, about to move, about to die) can have it moved there. Unmatched pushes this toward genuine control tempo by giving all Equipment in hand Reaction speed to attach — you can hold gear back and drop it in response to what the opponent does, rather than pre-committing and telegraphing the play. Unrelenting instead rewards re-attaching specifically onto himself, drawing a card each time — which synergizes directly with the Legend's own reattachment loop: move gear onto Unrelenting repeatedly, draw repeatedly.

This reads as a control-leaning value engine built around a durable, gear-carrying unit rather than either an aggro flood or a combo detonation — nothing here rewards board width or a specific assembled state, and nothing pays off in one explosive turn. Early game wants Equipment landed cheaply (Unrelenting's Weaponmaster discount on entry). Once running, the Legend ability keeps redirecting that gear to whichever unit is actually contesting a battlefield right now, with Unmatched allowing that redirection to happen at instant speed against the opponent's own plays, and each reattachment onto Unrelenting refunding a card to keep the loop going. The win condition is winning Showdowns outright with a persistently well-equipped unit, sustained turn over turn by the draw-on-attach rather than by one big payoff — attrition through equipment efficiency, not a burst.
