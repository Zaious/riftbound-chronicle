### Ornn - Fire Below the Mountain (Calm+Mind)

> Tier 2-confirmed PARTIALLY CONFIRMED 2026-08-18 (`references/verification-log.md`) — real coverage (riftbound.cardsrealm.com's "Voltron Blue Ornn" deck tech, 2026-01-28; hextechanalytics.com) confirms Ornn, Forge God as the real default Champion and confirms the gear-stacking-into-a-scaling-threat win condition, but Ornn, Blacksmith is reported cut from competitive builds entirely ("left behind, not even included in the side deck as a possible turn two play") rather than the central, repeating engine this entry treats it as — the real engine is Forge God's stat lead doubled by the equipment-doubler Svellsongur. Real sources also don't call this "Control": Cardsrealm labels it **Voltron** ("similarly to a Kai'Sa, but green and centered around Gears instead of spells"), hextechanalytics labels it **Combo/Engine-focused midrange**.

Legend ability: `Exhaust: Reaction — Add a rainbow rune. Use only to play gear or use gear abilities.` **Ornn - Blacksmith** (Calm): `When you play me or when I hold, look at the top 4 cards of your Main Deck. You may reveal a gear from among them and draw it. Then recycle the rest.` **Ornn - Forge God** (Mind): `Deflect 2. Weaponmaster. I have +1 Might for each friendly gear.` (Real decks build almost exclusively around Forge God — see the callout above; Blacksmith is a text-plausible engine piece that current competitive lists don't actually run.)

The Legend ability is a Reaction, meaning its rainbow-rune generation is available at flexible, as-needed timing rather than tied to a specific phase — it exists to keep gear plays and gear abilities affordable whenever the opportunity comes up, a resource-smoothing tool rather than a burst payoff. Blacksmith's trigger reads as the clearest signal in the packet from the text alone: it fires both on-play and "when I hold" — the latter looks like an explicitly repeating, ongoing value engine that only keeps paying out as long as Ornn's Battlefield stays held over multiple turns, a direct match for a control read (grinding out value by holding a point over time). Real play doesn't bear this out, though — competitive lists cut Blacksmith and build around Forge God from the start. Forge God converts accumulated gear into a scaling stat lead — +1 might per friendly gear, plus a Weaponmaster discount to equip cheaply the turn he lands — and real decks push this further than the text alone suggests, doubling the gear bonuses with Svellsongur to make Forge God himself "ridiculously, needlessly, and stupidly large" (Cardsrealm) while holding a single Battlefield behind a defensive spell suite (Zhonya's Hourglass, Guardian Angel, Defy, Wind Wall). Early turns are about getting Ornn onto a Battlefield and holding it cheaply (the Legend ability keeps gear affordable reactively even off-curve) while cheap gear-fetching pieces set up the equipment count; once Forge God lands, real lists stack gear and the Svellsongur doubler onto him directly rather than relying on Blacksmith's repeating draw. This converts to points in two stages: the hold itself denies the opponent a Battlefield directly, and the accumulated gear/might on Forge God eventually lets Ornn win Showdowns outright rather than merely survive them, once enough turns have passed to build the stack.
