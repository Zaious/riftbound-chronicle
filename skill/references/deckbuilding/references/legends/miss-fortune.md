### Miss Fortune - Bounty Hunter (Body+Chaos)

> Tier 2-confirmed NOT CONFIRMED 2026-08-18 (`references/verification-log.md`) — real competitive coverage identifies this as a Dazzling Aurora ramp/disruption deck ("Our main win condition is Dazzling Aurora" — Cardsrealm deck tech of a PPG Summit Orlando first-place list) that ramps into oversized off-kit units (Volibear, Imposing; Deadbloom Predator; Mountain Drake) and strips the opponent's hand (Mindsplitter, Sabotage), using Ganking mainly as a late finishing tool — not the token-swarm, claim-every-open-battlefield aggro deck this entry derives from the printed kit. Miss Fortune, Captain is confirmed the real default Champion (CORE, 84.62% presence, riftDecks.com), but Miss Fortune, Buccaneer — the card this entry leans on most heavily for its "no beachhead needed" aggro read — is barely played (FLEX, 7.69% presence), and riftbound.gg states outright that the aggressive open-battlefield build Buccaneer would enable "has yet to make it on the competitive stage." Currently Tier 5 and declining (August 2026 tier list).

Legend ability: `Exhaust: Give a unit Ganking this turn.` **Miss Fortune - Captain** (Body): `Accelerate. Ganking. The first time I move each turn, you may ready something else that's exhausted.` **Miss Fortune - Buccaneer** (Chaos): `You may play me to an open battlefield. Friendly units may be played to open battlefields.`

Every piece here is immediate on paper. The Legend ability itself is a flat activated effect with no condition beyond exhausting — grant any unit Ganking this turn, on demand, to redirect it to wherever the opponent is weak. Captain compounds that same turn: Accelerate lets her enter already-readied (able to act the turn she's played, not next turn), she already has Ganking innately, and the first move each turn lets you ready something else exhausted — meaning a single mobility play can chain into freeing up a second unit to also act, all in one turn. Buccaneer strips away the normal requirement of establishing a beachhead first: both she and any friendly unit can be played directly to an open (uncontested) Battlefield.

Real competitive play does not build this into the token-swarm, claim-every-open-battlefield aggro deck that reading of the printed kit suggests. The archetype that actually wins — independently confirmed by a PPG Summit Orlando first-place decklist (Cardsrealm, published 2025-12-18) and by riftbound.gg's own guide — is a Dazzling Aurora ramp deck: Mobilize and Catalyst of Aeons ramp runes toward Dazzling Aurora on turn 3-4, which then free-rolls oversized units (Volibear, Imposing; Deadbloom Predator; Mountain Drake) into play at the end of every turn, while Chaos/Body hand-disruption (Mindsplitter, Sabotage, Invert Timelines) strips the opponent's answers and win conditions before they can stabilize. "Our main win condition is Dazzling Aurora," not the Legend's own text. Captain is confirmed the real default Champion — riftbound.gg calls her "the typically chosen champion unit" and she sits at CORE, 84.62% presence — and her role matches this entry's read: moving to ready an already-landed giant unit so it can attack again, or chaining into freeing up a second attacker. But Buccaneer, the card this entry leans on most for the "no beachhead needed" aggro identity, is barely played in practice (FLEX, 7.69% presence); riftbound.gg calls her "the lesser used" Champion and states plainly that the aggressive open-battlefield build she would enable "has yet to make it on the competitive stage." Ganking itself is real and does matter, but mainly as a late-game closer — letting one ramped-in giant contest a second, undefended battlefield to end the game — rather than as the engine for an early aggressive land-grab.

Points come from Dazzling Aurora's ramped-in threats overpowering the board while hand disruption denies the opponent's answers and win conditions, with Ganking closing the game out by letting one big unit contest two battlefields in a single turn once the opponent is already behind — this is ramp-and-disrupt, not conquest-by-volume-and-speed aggro.
