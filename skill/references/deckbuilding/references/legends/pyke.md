### Pyke - Bloodharbor Ripper (Fury+Chaos)

Legend ability: `1 Energy, Exhaust: Return a friendly unit at a battlefield to its owner's hand. Play a Gold gear token exhausted.` **Pyke - Dockside Butcher** (Fury): `Hidden. Ganking. You may pay a Fury rune as an additional cost to play me. When you play me, if you paid the additional cost, ready me and give me +2 Might this turn.` **Pyke - Returned** (Chaos): `Hidden. Backline. Once each turn, when an enemy unit dies while I'm at a battlefield, play a Gold gear token exhausted.`

The Legend ability is a flexible, activated bounce: return a friendly unit from a Battlefield to hand and bank a Gold gear token that can later be cracked for a rainbow rune. That's a tempo tool with no single fixed direction — it can pull a unit out of a losing Showdown defensively, or reset it to re-trigger an on-play effect offensively, while incidentally stockpiling a convertible resource either way. Both Champions carry Hidden, a surprise-timing mechanic (cheap now, free reaction later) that's inherently about ambush tempo rather than telegraphed commitment. From there the two diverge sharply. Dockside Butcher is immediate and aggressive: pay extra Fury on play to enter already-readied with +2 might that turn, plus innate Ganking to redirect that burst to whichever Battlefield needs it — force a decision the instant he lands. Returned is patient and attritional instead: Backline keeps him protected (hit last in combat), and once each turn any enemy unit dying at his Battlefield mints another Gold gear token — a passive resource engine that rewards prolonged fighting at one held point rather than winning it outright. This is a genuine fork rather than a single lane: the Legend's own bounce-and-bank ability is tempo-neutral, Dockside Butcher commits the deck to aggro (ambush in, buff up, redirect via Ganking, force the Showdown now), and Returned commits it to control (sit protected on a contested point, farm resources off attrition, let the fight drag). Points diverge accordingly — the Butcher build wins Showdowns directly through ambush timing plus a same-turn might spike and mobility to the right target, while the Returned build converts sustained combat at a single held Battlefield into a growing bank of Gold gear/rainbow resources that indirectly fund whatever else the deck needs to keep holding elsewhere.
