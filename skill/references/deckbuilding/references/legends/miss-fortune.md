### Miss Fortune - Bounty Hunter (Body+Chaos)

Legend ability: `Exhaust: Give a unit Ganking this turn.` **Miss Fortune - Captain** (Body): `Accelerate. Ganking. The first time I move each turn, you may ready something else that's exhausted.` **Miss Fortune - Buccaneer** (Chaos): `You may play me to an open battlefield. Friendly units may be played to open battlefields.`

Every piece here is immediate. The Legend ability itself is a flat activated effect with no condition beyond exhausting — grant any unit Ganking this turn, on demand, to redirect it to wherever the opponent is weak. Captain compounds that same turn: Accelerate lets her enter already-readied (able to act the turn she's played, not next turn), she already has Ganking innately, and the first move each turn lets you ready something else exhausted — meaning a single mobility play can chain into freeing up a second unit to also act, all in one turn. Buccaneer strips away the normal requirement of establishing a beachhead first: both she and any friendly unit can be played directly to an open (uncontested) Battlefield, which is explicitly about claiming ground immediately rather than building up at a home base and marching out. None of this rewards patience or a held-back setup — it's entirely about forcing decisions the instant it resolves. That's an unambiguous aggro commitment per the framework: flood outward and force the opponent to react before they've stabilized. Early game for this deck is about deploying directly onto open Battlefields (Buccaneer) rather than contesting from a single anchor point; once the engine is running, Ganking (Legend ability plus Captain's innate copy) redirects pressure to whichever point just opened up or went weak, and Captain's ready-something-else clause means one mobility play can effectively generate a second unit's worth of action that turn. Points come from winning the race to claim multiple Battlefields, many of them uncontested via Buccaneer's open-battlefield deployment, before the opponent can concentrate defense — this is conquest-by-volume-and-speed, not a grind and not a single big swing.
