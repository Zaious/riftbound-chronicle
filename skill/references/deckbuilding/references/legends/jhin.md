### Jhin - Virtuoso (Fury+Mind)

> Tier 2-confirmed NOT CONFIRMED 2026-08-18 (`references/verification-log.md`) — not a real split. Meticulous Killer is CORE in real decks (~84% presence, and *above*-average performance where it appears); Murderous Artist appears in roughly 11% of Jhin decks and *underperforms* when it does. Murderous Artist is a genuinely well-played card overall, but real data shows it's mostly splashed into other Fury-aggro Legends (Akali, Draven, Vi, Lucian) — it's a good card, just not really Jhin's own rival archetype.

Legend ability: `When you play a spell, if you spent 4+ Energy, you may banish it. Then, if there are four spells banished with me, put each in its trash, channel 4 runes, and draw 1.` Champions: **Jhin - Murderous Artist** (Fury) — `Deflect. Ganking. When I move, add 1 Energy and a rainbow rune.` **Jhin - Meticulous Killer** (Mind) — `Vision. If you've spent 4+ Energy to play a spell this turn, you may play me for a Mind rune.`

The Legend ability is a slow-burn counting combo: every time you cast a spell costing 4+ Energy, you may bank it (banished, not trashed), and only once four such spells have accumulated does it detonate — trashing all four, channeling 4 runes, and drawing a card. That's about as explicit a "patient setup, then one big payoff" trigger as exists in this pool: it requires four separate expensive-spell castings across a game before it fires once. Meticulous Killer feeds this plan directly — if you've already spent 4+ Energy on a spell that turn, he comes down for just a Mind rune, letting the same big-spell turn also deploy your Champion for free. Murderous Artist's mobile, move-triggered resource kit reads as textually plausible for a faster, tempo-forward alternative — but real decks don't build around it as a rival Jhin archetype; it's mainly a strong card in *other* Legends' aggro shells. Treat Meticulous Killer as this Legend's real identity, not one option in an even fork.
