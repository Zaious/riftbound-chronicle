### Renata Glasc - Chem-Baroness (Mind+Order)

Legend ability: `When you or an ally hold, you may exhaust me to play a Gold gear token exhausted. While your score is within 3 points of the Victory Score, your Gold gear tokens add an additional Energy.` **Renata Glasc - Mastermind** (Mind): `1 Energy + a Mind rune: Draw 1. 4 Energy + 4 Mind runes, Exhaust: Score 1 point. Use my abilities only while I'm at a battlefield.` **Renata Glasc - Industrialist** (Order): `Your tokens enter ready.`

Archetype: control. The Legend's trigger is hold, not conquer — it rewards a battlefield you already possess and are sustaining, not the moment of capture, which is the reactive/conditional-on-persistence timing that signals a grind plan rather than a race. The scaling clause ("while your score is within 3 of Victory") is explicitly an endgame accelerant: the closer the game is to ending, the more Energy the deck's own gear economy produces, which only matters to a deck planning to still be playing a long game at that point.

Core unfolding: early game is about planting units and establishing holds rather than forcing fights — every turn spent holding mints another Gold gear token, and Industrialist makes that token (and any other tokens) enter ready instead of exhausted, so its resource-adding reaction is live the same turn it appears. Once the engine is running, Mastermind sits at a battlefield converting banked Mind runes into a steady card-draw drip, then, once enough Energy and rune investment has accumulated, can pay a large activation cost to score a point directly — a second, combat-independent path to points layered on top of holding. As the game nears the Victory Score, the Legend's own bonus Energy from Gold gear compounds, making the late-game turns where this deck is strongest exactly the turns it was built to reach.

Win condition: primarily holding uncontested to keep the hold-trigger firing and the gear economy running, with Mastermind offering a genuine secondary path of spending accumulated resources to score directly rather than through combat or capture at all — an unusually explicit resource-to-points conversion for a control shell.
