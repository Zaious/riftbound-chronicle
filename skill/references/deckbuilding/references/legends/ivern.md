### Ivern - Green Father (Calm+Order)

> Tier 2-confirmed CONFIRMED 2026-08-18 (`references/verification-log.md`) — two independent deck-tech sources describe a control shell built around holding + Brush conversion, matching this entry's read directly. Some real sources use "midrange" instead of "control," but the entry's own hedged language ("control-shaped... combo-style assembly requirement") already accommodates either label, so no rewrite was needed.

Legend ability: `When you conquer or hold, you may exhaust me to replace that battlefield with a Brush battlefield token. (Bird, Cat, Dog, Poro, and Ivern units have +1 Might in Brush. It can be swapped back when scored.)` Champions: **Ivern - Nurturer** (Calm) — `When you play me or when I hold, look at the top 3 cards of your Main Deck. You may reveal a unit from among them and draw it. Recycle the rest. Then if you revealed a Bird, Cat, Dog, or Poro, buff a friendly unit.` **Ivern - Friend to All** (Order) — `As you play me, choose Bird, Cat, Dog, or Poro. I gain that tag. When I conquer or hold, score 1 point if your units have all of the following tags among them — Bird, Cat, Dog, and Poro.`

The Legend ability fires on conquer or hold — notably including hold, the passive "still there next turn" state, not just the active moment of taking a battlefield — and converts that territory into permanently upgraded ground (Brush) for a specific tag family. That's a control-shaped trigger: it rewards sustained possession, not a single burst of aggression. Nurturer reinforces the patience — it digs on play and again every time you hold, filtering toward a unit and conditionally buffing if the found card carries one of the four tags, which is consistency support for a tag-diverse build rather than raw stats. Friend to All is the sharpest structural signal: it needs all four tags — Bird, Cat, Dog, and Poro — present among your units simultaneously before it scores a point on conquer or hold. That's a genuine combo-style assembly requirement layered on top of a control-style holding shell: you must collect a specific, varied board composition before the bonus point fires, and it won't fire on turn one no matter how aggressively you play.

Early game is spent using Nurturer's dig to find and deploy tagged units, building toward having one of each of the four tags on board. Once a battlefield is being held, the Legend's Brush conversion permanently buffs whichever tagged units sit there, and if the four-tag set is complete, Friend to All adds a bonus point on top of the hold itself. The close-out is not a race — it's an accumulating value engine on top of already-held ground. The win condition is primarily indirect-to-direct via holding: Brush's stat buff helps the units already sitting on a battlefield keep winning or surviving Showdowns there over time, with Friend to All layering a secondary, conditional direct point once the tag collection is assembled — resource/composition-driven, not a burst.
