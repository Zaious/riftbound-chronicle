### Renekton - Butcher of the Sands (Fury+Body)

Legend ability: `Reaction: two rainbow runes, Exhaust: Add 2 Energy. Spend this Energy only to play units or activated abilities of units.` **Renekton, Rage Fueled** (Fury): `Accelerate. When I attack, if you control 4 or fewer runes, deal 2 to all enemy units here.` **Renekton, Brute** (Body): `1 Energy: Give me +1 might this turn. When my Might becomes 10 or more, empower me. Empowered: I have Deflect and Ganking.`

Archetype: genuinely split, and the Legend ability itself is neutral — a Reaction that converts spare runes into Energy for units, useful to either plan and not diagnostic on its own. The two Champions pull in different directions. Rage Fueled's payoff explicitly requires low rune count (4 or fewer) — since runes accumulate as a game goes on, this is a clock that only rewards early turns, gating an AOE wipe behind attacking before the board matures. That's an aggro commitment with a built-in expiration date. Brute instead wants sustained Energy investment turn after turn to climb to 10 Might, at which point it becomes a hard-to-remove, mobile threat (Deflect + Ganking) — a durable single-threat plan built around surviving and scaling, not racing.

Core unfolding: with Rage Fueled, the deck wants to attack immediately and repeatedly while rune count is still low, using the AOE clear to open battlefields before the opponent's board (or your own rune bank) grows past the threshold — this is a short-clock plan that gets structurally worse the longer the game runs. With Brute, the deck instead wants to keep feeding Energy into one body across several turns, tolerating a slower start because the payoff (Deflect+Ganking on an already-big unit) is a threat built to survive removal and reposition across battlefields once it arrives.

Win condition: Rage Fueled's plan is to win Showdowns outright via AOE-cleared blockers while runes are still low. Brute's plan is closer to holding uncontested — once Empowered, a Deflect-protected, mobile 10+-Might unit is built to plant itself somewhere and be very hard to dislodge or answer. This is a Legend where the Champion choice, not the Legend ability, decides the archetype.
