### Master Yi - Wuju Bladesman (Calm+Body)

> Tier 2-confirmed 2026-08-17 (`references/verification-log.md`) — the original entry presented Meditative and Honed as a genuine fork into two comparably-weighted lanes. Real practice has converged on one dominant, extensively-proven strategy instead (Top 8 at every Unleashed Regional Qualifier as of verification), built on Ambush synergy with cards *outside* this Legend+Champion kit — a real fork is not what's happening here. Also confirmed: this identity has held from Origins through Spiritforged and Unleashed without shifting, so it doesn't depend on a set-exclusive combo piece — buildable wherever Master Yi himself is legal, Taiwan's current OGN pool included.

Legend ability: `While a friendly unit defends alone, it gets +2 Might.` **Master Yi - Meditative** (Calm): `While you have 8+ runes, I have +4 Might.` **Master Yi - Honed** (Body): `Ganking. I enter ready.`

The Legend ability is a standing condition, not a trigger with a timing window at all — it's always live whenever a single friendly unit is defending a Battlefield alone, which structurally rewards thin, efficient defense over stacking bodies at every point: a solo-hold shell, not a wide board. Real practice confirms this is the actual, singular identity — "a representative solo-hold & Ambush-style control deck," where high Ambush-keyword synergy lets the deck ambush the opponent's additional deployments rather than needing to already have units in place to defend. Honed's Ganking-plus-ready serves that same solo-hold plan rather than defining a competing aggressive lane: mobility here is what lets Yi (or a defender) redeploy to wherever the single-defended point needs reinforcing, or what makes him a strong Ambush target himself, not a pivot into contesting multiple Battlefields at once. Meditative's rune-hoarding condition is a second lever on the identical plan — a deck that's already committed to patiently holding one point is naturally the deck that isn't spending its runes down turn over turn, so the resource-patience reward and the solo-hold plan reinforce each other rather than representing a different game entirely.
