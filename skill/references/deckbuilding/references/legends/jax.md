### Jax - Grandmaster At Arms (Calm+Body)

> Tier 2-confirmed PARTIALLY CONFIRMED 2026-08-18 (`references/verification-log.md`) — the mechanical read (Equipment redeployment, draw-on-attach with Unrelenting) and the "attrition through equipment efficiency, not a burst" win condition both hold up. The archetype label doesn't: two independent named sources classify this deck as **midrange**, not control, one explicitly describing a "pretty slow but quite explosive... mid game" phase — an offensive/conquering stretch that doesn't fit a reactive-control shell. A separate ranking source flags the deck as "sluggish" due to once-per-turn triggers, a real viability caveat this entry didn't carry.

Legend ability: `1 Energy, Exhaust: Attach a detached Equipment you control to a unit you control. Exhaust: Attach an attached Equipment you control to a unit you control.` Champions: **Jax - Unmatched** (Calm) — `Deflect. Each Equipment in your hand has Quick-Draw. (It gains Reaction. When you play it, attach it to a unit you control.)` **Jax - Unrelenting** (Body) — `Weaponmaster. When you attach an Equipment to me, you may pay 1 Energy to draw 1.`

The Legend ability is a plain repeatable activated ability — no opponent-dependent condition, no burst window — that redeploys Equipment between units at will. That's a flexibility/resilience tool rather than a race or setup-payoff mechanic: whichever unit currently needs the gear (about to fight, about to move, about to die) can have it moved there. Unmatched pushes this toward instant-speed flexibility by giving all Equipment in hand Reaction speed to attach — you can hold gear back and drop it in response to what the opponent does, rather than pre-committing and telegraphing the play. Unrelenting instead rewards re-attaching specifically onto himself, drawing a card each time — which synergizes directly with the Legend's own reattachment loop: move gear onto Unrelenting repeatedly, draw repeatedly.

Real coverage frames this as a **midrange** value engine, not control — a slower start that opens into a genuinely offensive mid-game push once Equipment value has accumulated, before settling into the attrition-based holding this entry's mechanical read already captures well. Early game wants Equipment landed cheaply (Unrelenting's Weaponmaster discount on entry). Once running, the Legend ability keeps redirecting that gear to whichever unit is actually contesting a battlefield right now, with Unmatched allowing that redirection to happen at instant speed against the opponent's own plays, and each reattachment onto Unrelenting refunding a card to keep the loop going. The win condition is winning Showdowns outright with a persistently well-equipped unit, sustained turn over turn by the draw-on-attach rather than by one big payoff — attrition through equipment efficiency, not a burst.
