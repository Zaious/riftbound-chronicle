Legend ability: `While a friendly unit defends alone, it gets +2 Might.` **Master Yi - Meditative** (Calm): `While you have 8+ runes, I have +4 Might.` **Master Yi - Honed** (Body): `Ganking. I enter ready.`

The Legend ability is a standing condition, not a trigger with a timing window at all — it's always live whenever a single friendly unit is defending a Battlefield alone, which structurally rewards thin, efficient defense over stacking bodies at every point: a solo-hold shell, not a wide board. Real practice confirms this is the actual, singular identity — one of the best Legends in the game for Ambush synergy specifically, where high Ambush-keyword density lets the deck contest the opponent's additional deployments rather than needing to already have units in place to defend. Honed's Ganking-plus-ready serves that same solo-hold plan rather than defining a competing aggressive lane: mobility here is what lets Yi (or a defender) redeploy to wherever the single-defended point needs reinforcing, or what makes him a strong Ambush target himself, not a pivot into contesting multiple Battlefields at once. Meditative's rune-hoarding condition is a second lever on the identical plan — a deck that's already committed to patiently holding one point is naturally the deck that isn't spending its runes down turn over turn, so the resource-patience reward and the solo-hold plan reinforce each other rather than representing a different game entirely. **What's confirmed as evergreen (the solo-hold identity itself) and what's a snapshot of the current top-performing build (which specific Unleashed-era Ambush support cards make it Top-8-caliber right now) are two different kinds of claim — don't quote the second kind as if it were as durable as the first.**
