### Rek'Sai - Void Burrower (Fury+Order)

> Tier 2-confirmed PARTIALLY CONFIRMED 2026-08-18 (`references/verification-log.md`) — the conquer-into-reveal-2-play-1 chaining win condition is confirmed almost verbatim by hextechanalytics.com and riftstorm.gg, and Rek'Sai, Breacher is confirmed the real dominant Champion (330+ decks/month vs. Swarm Queen's ~7, per riftDecks.com), but two independent real sources explicitly deny the "Archetype: aggro" label below — riftstorm.gg (2026-07-06): "Rek'Sai is not the mindless Fury aggro deck the domain colors suggest," calling it "off-tempo" and "one of the trickiest tempo legends in the format"; hextechanalytics.com labels it "Midrange" outright and names Breacher the "Chosen Champion."

> Errata-corrected 2026-08-17, second pass 2026-08-18 (`references/errata_overlay.json`, Spiritforged Errata, 2026-01-14) — both the Legend ability (errata'd under the card's own title, "Void Burrower") and Swarm Queen's ability reflect the official wording fix below. A re-audit caught that the first pass fixed only Swarm Queen's text and missed that "Void Burrower" is this Legend's own title, not a separate card — the Legend ability line still had 2026-08-17's pre-errata wording until this second pass. No mechanical change from the pre-errata text in either case; the analysis is unaffected.

Legend ability: `When you conquer, you may exhaust me to reveal the top 2 cards of your Main Deck. You may banish one, then play it. Recycle the rest.` **Rek'Sai - Breacher** (Fury): `Accelerate. Assault. Friendly units played from anywhere other than a player's hand have Accelerate.` **Rek'Sai - Swarm Queen** (Order): `When I attack, you may reveal the top 2 cards of your Main Deck. You may banish one, then play it. If it is a unit, you may play it here. Recycle the rest.` (Real decks build almost entirely around Breacher — see the callout above; Swarm Queen is a real but essentially unplayed print.)

Archetype: aggro by text derivation, though real coverage pushes back on that label specifically — see the callout above; "off-tempo" or "midrange" is the real community language. The Legend's trigger is conquer itself — the same act that scores — so the ability doesn't wait for a setup turn or a resource threshold, it fires the instant you win a battlefield and immediately hands you more material to keep pushing. That card-advantage loop is exactly what real coverage credits as the reason this deck "does not run out of gas" the way a pure aggro shell would. Swarm Queen moves that same reveal-2-play-1 effect even earlier, onto the attack step, so the deck is digging for extra bodies before the fight is even resolved — a real, legal interaction, but current competitive lists overwhelmingly favor Breacher instead. Neither Champion asks you to wait or bank anything; both convert combat actions directly into more combat actions.

Core unfolding: early game wants Rek'Sai attacking battlefields as soon as it's down, since Swarm Queen's attack-trigger alone starts generating extra units before any conquest happens. Breacher's blanket Accelerate for anything played outside the hand means units surfaced by either reveal effect land ready to fight immediately rather than sitting a turn — the deck never has to pause to let newly-found material catch up. Once conquering starts, the Legend ability re-triggers the same dig, so each captured battlefield refuels the next attack rather than ending the turn's momentum. There's no distinct "engine spin-up" phase separate from the aggression — the card advantage is generated by attacking, not by a separate setup investment.

Win condition: winning Showdowns outright and chaining them across multiple battlefields. The reveal-and-play effects are pure fuel for continued attacking, not a holding or resource-stockpiling plan — every point of card advantage this Legend generates is spent immediately re-entering combat rather than banked toward a later turn.
