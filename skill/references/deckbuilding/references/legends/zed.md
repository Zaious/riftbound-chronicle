### Zed, Master of Shadows (Fury+Chaos)

> Tier 2-confirmed NOT CONFIRMED 2026-08-18 (`references/verification-log.md`) — not a real aggro/combo fork. A dedicated card-kit review states directly that the two Champions are "complementary pieces of one unified deck, not separate archetypes," both supporting the same core Shadow-Clone-token-and-discard loop — and real tournament data backs this: both Champion cards are typically run together in the same list, and the community's own name for the archetype (current Vendetta-era events) is **"Miracle Zed,"** a single named combo deck, not an aggro-lane/combo-lane split.

Legend ability: `When you banish a card you own, empower me. Action: Disempower me, Exhaust: Discard 1, then draw 1.` **Zed, From the Shadows** (Fury): `You may discard 1 as an additional cost to play me. When you play me, if you paid the additional cost, play a 0 might Shadow Clone unit token. ("When I attack, you may banish a unit from your trash. If you do, give me Assault 4 this turn.")` **Zed, Without a Sound** (Chaos): `When I conquer, play a 0 might Shadow Clone unit token to your base. Action: 1 Energy and a Chaos rune: Move me and a Shadow Clone you control to each other's locations.`

The Legend ability rewards banishing your own cards with a loot effect (discard, then draw) — a consistency tool that keeps the hand fresh, feeding a single self-sustaining discard/banish loop rather than two separate plans. On paper, From the Shadows (an explosive Assault-4 swing off a banished trash unit) and Without a Sound (compounding Clone tokens plus repositioning) read as a burst-vs-grind split — but real deck construction runs both Champions' cards together as one combo shell built around Shadow Clone generation, not a fork where a deckbuilder commits to one lane. Real-play support cards cited alongside this core (community-named "Miracle Zed"): Shadow Order Disciple, Punching Poro, Kennen - Storm of Shurikens, Blade Twirler, Traveling Merchant.

**Core unfolding:** early turns discard to play Zed and feed the trash, spawning Shadow Clone tokens both on conquest (Without a Sound) and on entry (From the Shadows); the loop compounds as more Clones accumulate board presence and more banished trash cards fund Assault-4 swings, with the Legend's own loot ability keeping the hand stocked to keep feeding it. This is a genuine combo identity (a self-sustaining engine that gets more dangerous the longer it runs), assessed by real coverage as a competitive but not top-tier build ("maybe a tier 1 list or a high tier 2 list").

**Win condition:** winning Showdowns outright through accumulated Clone tokens and Assault-4-boosted swings from the same unified engine — not two different engines chosen at deckbuilding time.
