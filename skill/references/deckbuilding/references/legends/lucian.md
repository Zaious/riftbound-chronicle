### Lucian - Purifier (Fury+Body)

> Tier 2-confirmed PARTIALLY CONFIRMED 2026-08-18 (`references/verification-log.md`) — Lucian, Merciless is confirmed the real "Chosen Champion" (riftbound.gg) and CORE at 79.41% presence (riftDecks.com), matching this entry's read. But Lucian, Gunslinger — framed here as a comparably-weighted removal-generating partner — is essentially unplayed in practice: absent entirely from riftDecks.com's ~70 tracked cards for this Legend, and riftbound.gg states it "isn't seeing much play at the moment... too fragile outside being an attacker" and "will likely not be impactful." Real coverage also frames the deck less as pure aggro and more as a "conqueror type of legend" built on "strong midrange units" that has to protect its attackers to keep conquering turn after turn. Currently a fringe competitive pick (Tier 4, August 2026 tier list).

Legend ability: `Your Equipment each give Assault. (+1 might while equipped unit is an attacker.)` **Lucian - Gunslinger** (Fury): `Assault. When I attack, deal damage equal to my Assault to an enemy unit here.` **Lucian - Merciless** (Body): `Weaponmaster. The first time I conquer each turn, ready me.`

**Archetype**: Text-derivation called this Aggro, reasoning that the Legend ability's Assault buff only matters on offense. Real coverage (riftbound.gg) confirms the Equipment/conquest core but frames it less as an unconditional attrition race and more as a "conqueror type of legend" built around "strong midrange units": "the Purifier needs to conquer every turn, then keep its units safe even if it means the opponent gets to score, as their survival is crucial to conquer again the following turn" — protecting an attacker after the swing, not just flooding forward. Gunslinger, framed here as a comparably-weighted removal-generating partner to Merciless, is essentially unplayed in real decks: riftbound.gg says it "isn't seeing much play at the moment... too fragile outside being an attacker" and "will likely not be impactful," and it doesn't appear at all among riftDecks.com's ~70 tracked cards for this Legend. Merciless is the real, confirmed "Chosen Champion" (CORE, 79.41% presence) and matches this entry's read as a tempo piece: her on-play Equip discount gets a weapon attached the turn she lands, and "the first time I conquer each turn, ready me" lets her act again immediately after a successful conquest.

**Core unfolding**: Early game wants Equipment attached and attacking fast — Merciless's discounted Weaponmaster gets an armed attacker down the same turn she's played. Gunslinger's text would turn every attack into both bigger combat stats and a free damage ping against whatever's opposing it, but real decks rarely run her (see Tier 2 callout above); the actual engine runs on Merciless's conquer-then-ready, which extends a single turn's tempo by letting a successful attacker immediately follow up or reposition to defend newly taken ground. Nothing here scales explosively — no cost-reduction snowball, no delayed payoff — it's a steady per-turn Equipment-fueled conqueror plan.

**Engine to points**: A win-the-Showdown-by-attacking plan — Assault only functions on offense, Gunslinger's damage clears the way to conquest, and Merciless's extra readying exists to keep pressing rather than to generate cards. Nothing in this Legend's kit produces draw or independent resource advantage; the whole engine converts Equipment investment straight into successful attacks.
