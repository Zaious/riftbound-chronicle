### Volibear, Relentless Storm (Fury+Body)

Legend ability: `When you play a Mighty unit, you may exhaust me to channel 1 rune exhausted. (Mighty = 5+ might)` **Volibear, Furious** (Fury): `Deflect 2. When I attack, deal 5 damage split among any number of enemy units here.` **Volibear, Imposing** (Body): `Shield 3. Tank. When an opponent moves to a battlefield other than mine, draw 1.`

The Legend ability itself is archetype-neutral ramp: it only cares that you played a 5+ might unit, converting that into an extra resource. What that resource funds diverges completely between the two Champions. Furious is attack-triggered removal: 5 damage split across every enemy unit at the location on attack, clearing the way to force a conquer through — an aggressive tool. Imposing is a passive value engine keyed off the opponent's movement, not your own — a huge Shield/Tank body that punishes the opponent for playing around it by drawing you a card, which only matters if you intend to sit and hold rather than push. This is a genuine split: the same big-body resource requirement (get 5+ might units into play) funds two structurally opposite plans depending on the pick, so this Legend does not commit to a single lane — it's a Fury-aggro/Body-control fork built on a shared curve requirement.

Core unfolding, shared start: because the ramp trigger needs a Mighty (5+) unit already in play, the early turns are about hitting that threshold as soon as the curve allows — a deck too light on big bodies never gets to use the Legend ability at all. From there the paths split. With Furious, once a big attacker lands, its attack-triggered AoE clears every contesting unit at a location in one swing, directly forcing the conquer — points come from winning Showdowns outright via damage-based removal. With Imposing, the big body simply parks on a Battlefield; its stats are built to survive almost any combat it's forced into, and every attempt by the opponent to route around it feeds card draw — points come from holding a single Battlefield uncontested over time while the draw engine keeps refilling the hand.
