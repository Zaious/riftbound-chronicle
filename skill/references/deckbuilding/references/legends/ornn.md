### Ornn - Fire Below the Mountain (Calm+Mind)

Legend ability: `Exhaust: Reaction — Add a rainbow rune. Use only to play gear or use gear abilities.` **Ornn - Blacksmith** (Calm): `When you play me or when I hold, look at the top 4 cards of your Main Deck. You may reveal a gear from among them and draw it. Then recycle the rest.` **Ornn - Forge God** (Mind): `Deflect 2. Weaponmaster. I have +1 Might for each friendly gear.`

The Legend ability is a Reaction, meaning its rainbow-rune generation is available at flexible, as-needed timing rather than tied to a specific phase — it exists to keep gear plays and gear abilities affordable whenever the opportunity comes up, a resource-smoothing tool rather than a burst payoff. Blacksmith's trigger is the clearest signal in the packet: it fires both on-play and "when I hold" — the latter is an explicitly repeating, ongoing value engine that only keeps paying out as long as Ornn's Battlefield stays held over multiple turns. That's a direct match for the control definition (grinding out value by holding a point over time), not a one-shot. Forge God then converts whatever gear that engine accumulates into a scaling stat lead — +1 might per friendly gear, plus a Weaponmaster discount to equip cheaply the turn he lands — turning the passive card-selection engine into an actual combat threat as the game goes long. Early turns are about getting Ornn onto a Battlefield and holding it cheaply (the Legend ability keeps gear affordable reactively even off-curve); once "when I hold" starts recurring, Blacksmith is filtering into more gear every turn that Battlefield stays yours, and Forge God (if that's the Champion in play) is stacking that gear into raw might. This converts to points in two stages: the repeating hold-trigger rewards uncontested or successfully-defended ground directly, and the accumulated gear/might eventually lets Ornn win Showdowns outright rather than merely survive them, once enough turns have passed to build the stack.
