### Fiora - Grand Duelist (Body+Order)

Legend ability: `When one of your units becomes Mighty, you may exhaust me to channel 1 rune exhausted. (A unit is Mighty while it has 5+ Might.)` Champions: **Fiora - Peerless** (Body) — `When I attack or defend one on one, double my Might this combat.` **Fiora - Worthy** (Order) — `When a unit you control becomes Mighty, you may pay an Order rune to ready it.`

The Legend ability doesn't fire on a fixed timing — it fires on a state change, "becomes Mighty," meaning it's dormant until a unit crosses the 5+ Might line, then pays out a rune. That's a threshold-crossing trigger, and both Champions are built to make crossings happen repeatedly and matter more when they do. Peerless doubles her own Might in a 1v1 combat — a single Showdown can push her from below 5 to comfortably above it mid-fight, meaning the "becomes Mighty" event can happen inside combat itself rather than needing pre-buffing. Worthy converts every such crossing into a readied unit — an extra action, not just a resource.

That combination — an in-combat stat-double that can trip the threshold, paired with a Champion that turns each trip into an untapped unit — is a combo shape: the deck wants to set up a 1v1 Showdown where Fiora crosses 5+ Might, which simultaneously channels a rune (Legend) and readies her (Worthy), letting her act again — attack elsewhere, defend, or simply be available for the next threshold event — inside the same turn. This isn't aggro's board-flood (nothing here rewards adding bodies) and it isn't control's slow denial (nothing here removes or holds space passively); it's a patient build toward a specific stat line that, once hit, cascades into extra actions. Early game is about getting one or two units to the doorstep of 5 Might cheaply — Peerless's own doubling is the cheapest route, needing only a 1v1 fight rather than external buffs. Once running, each Mighty crossing both ramps resources (rune channel) and re-enables combat (Worthy's ready), which converts directly into winning Showdowns outright — a readied Fiora can contest another battlefield or defend the one she just took, so the payoff is repeated combat wins funded by her own stat growth, not a resource stockpile cashed in later.
