### Teemo, Swift Scout (Mind+Chaos)

> Errata-corrected 2026-08-17 (`references/errata_overlay.json`, Origins Card Errata, 2025-10-28) — Strategist's ability text below reflects the official correction: the "or I'm played from Hidden" trigger clause was removed (defend-only now), and the damage was changed from a loose "an enemy unit here" to an explicitly chosen single target. The original entry was written against the pre-errata text; the analysis below has been re-derived from the corrected wording, not just re-quoted.
>
> Tier 2-confirmed PARTIALLY CONFIRMED 2026-08-18 (`references/verification-log.md`) — the two Champions really do pull differently on paper, but this isn't a clean, currently-comparable fork. Real coverage: "there is only one list of Teemo with a strong performance at the moment," built on Strategist. Scout's own results are real but stale — they're from an earlier (Origins-format) meta, and current coverage says Scout "was unable to post the same results" as the format moved on. Some real tournament lists don't pick one Champion exclusively either — a Sydney RQ Top 8 list ran copies of both Strategist and Scout in the same deck, a different shape than "fork into two decks" entirely.

Legend ability: `You may pay 1 Energy to hide a card with Hidden instead of a rainbow rune. 1 Energy, Exhaust: Put a Teemo unit you own into your hand from your Champion Zone or the board.` **Teemo, Strategist** (Mind): `Hidden... When I defend, choose an enemy unit here and reveal the top 5 cards of your Main Deck. Deal 1 to that unit for each card with Hidden revealed this way, then recycle the revealed cards.` **Teemo, Scout** (Chaos): `Hidden... When you play me, give me +3 might this turn.`

The Legend ability is an enabler, not a payoff: it makes Hidden cheaper to activate and lets you recycle a Teemo body back to hand for repeated re-deployment. That recursion is archetype-neutral by itself — what it feeds differs sharply by Champion. Strategist's trigger is now purely reactive — defend only, since errata removed the earlier "or I'm played from Hidden" clause — and its payoff scales with how many Hidden cards are stacked in the deck at reveal time, but it must be aimed: the corrected text requires choosing one enemy unit up front and stacking all the revealed damage onto that single target, rather than the pre-errata wording's looser "an enemy unit here." That's a control tool with a removal-shaped teeth: sit back, let Teemo defend, and use the reveal to pick off one specific blocker or threat rather than spread chip damage. Scout's trigger is immediate-on-play, a flat +3 tempo burst with no dependency on deck state — this is straightforward aggro pressure. The two Champions genuinely pull toward different lanes off the same chassis; the Legend ability's retrieval loop just lets whichever plan you pick reuse the same unit's trigger multiple times rather than deciding the lane itself.

Core unfolding depends on the pick. With Strategist, the deck wants a critical mass of Hidden cards seeded early (cheap via the Legend's energy-alternative payment) so that by the time Teemo is forced to defend, the top-5 reveal is dense with Hidden hits and the single chosen target takes a big hit — a grind that improves as the game goes on, converting into points by surviving attacks and holding Battlefields rather than pushing them. Because the trigger is defend-only post-errata, this Champion specifically wants to be attacked into, not to initiate — a genuinely passive, bait-and-punish shape rather than a flexible "defend or unhide on my schedule" one. With Scout, the deck wants Teemo hidden and popped up as fast as possible for the +3 might spike, then the retrieval ability recurs him to do it again — converting into points by winning individual Showdowns outright through repeated burst attacks, racing before the opponent's board stabilizes.
