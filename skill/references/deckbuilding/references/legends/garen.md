### Garen - Might of Demacia (Body+Order)

Legend ability: `When you conquer, if you have 4+ units at that battlefield, draw 2.` Champions: **Garen - Rugged** (Body) — `Assault 2, Shield 2 (+2 Might while I'm an attacker or defender).` **Garen - Commander** (Order) — `Other friendly units have +1 Might here.`

The trigger is immediate and on-the-spot — the moment you conquer, not end of turn, not reactive — but it's gated on a hard board-width requirement: 4+ units already stacked at that one battlefield. That's an aggro condition in the most literal sense the framework describes: force a single battlefield to a numbers head before the opponent can match it, and get burst-refueled (draw 2) for succeeding. Commander's aura directly supports the pile-up, buffing every other friendly unit at that same battlefield — so going wide at one location isn't just for the draw trigger, it's also how the stack actually wins the fight. Rugged makes Garen himself efficient in both directions (Assault 2 attacking, Shield 2 defending), so he can lead the initial push and then help the stacked board hold the location afterward rather than being a one-way beater.

Early game wants multiple cheap units converging on the same battlefield rather than spread across three — the payoff specifically needs 4+ bodies at the location being conquered, so width concentrated in one place matters more than width in general. Once the first stacked conquest lands, the draw-2 refuels the hand for the next wave, letting the deck keep re-flooding a battlefield rather than running out of gas after one push — the classic wide-aggro sustain loop. The close is direct: Commander's buffed stack and Rugged's Shield 2 aren't just for winning the initial Showdown, they're what lets the same pile of units hold the battlefield against the counter-push, so this Legend converts into points by winning Showdowns outright through numeric and stat superiority, then holding with the same bodies.
