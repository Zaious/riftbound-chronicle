Meditative's rune-hoarding condition is a second lever on the identical plan — a deck that's already committed to patiently holding one point is naturally the deck that isn't spending its runes down turn over turn, so the resource-patience reward and the solo-hold plan reinforce each other rather than representing a different game entirely — but Meditative itself is barely played; Unstoppable's cost-reduction leveling line instead anchors a real, separately-recognized secondary build ("Wuju Master") that trades the Tempered deck's consistency for a slower, more explosive XP payoff, at a real but clearly lower level of competitive success. **What's confirmed as evergreen (the solo-hold identity itself) and what's a snapshot of the current top-performing build (which specific Unleashed-era Ambush support cards, and now which specific Champion print, make it Top-8-caliber right now) are two different kinds of claim — don't quote the second kind as if it were as durable as the first.**
