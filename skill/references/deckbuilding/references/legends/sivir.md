### Sivir - Battle Mistress (Body+Chaos)

Legend ability: `When you recycle a rune, you may exhaust me to play a Gold gear token exhausted. When one or more enemy units die, ready me.` **Sivir - Ambitious** (Body): `Deflect 2. When I conquer after an attack, if you assigned 5 or more excess damage to enemy units, you may deal that much to an enemy unit.` **Sivir - Mercenary** (Chaos): `Accelerate. If you've spent at least two rainbow runes this turn, I have +2 might and Ganking.`

Archetype: aggro. The Legend has two reactive triggers — rune recycling (routine resource cycling) and enemy units dying (combat attrition) — and both fire more often the more actively a deck is initiating fights rather than sitting back. Ready-ing Sivir off enemy deaths specifically means the deck's own aggression is what keeps refueling the ability, not a passive clock.

Ambitious rewards attacking hard enough to generate 5+ excess damage, then redirects that overkill into a second kill on conquer — a combat-forward payoff that wants big attacking stats and multiple enemies stacked at a battlefield to spill damage into. Mercenary enters ready (Accelerate) and, on a turn where at least two rainbow runes have been spent, gains both a might boost and Ganking — rewarding a heavy-spending, active turn with a bigger, more mobile threat able to immediately press elsewhere. Neither Champion has a setup-and-wait clause; both convert an aggressive turn's activity directly into more pressure.

Core unfolding: early game wants Sivir attacking to start racking up excess damage and kills, which both readies the Legend for more Gold gear generation and, with Ambitious, immediately redirects spare damage into clearing a second blocker. Once running, the deck wants to keep spending heavily and initiating fights every turn — heavy spending empowers Mercenary's stat and mobility bonus, and continued kills keep the Legend's readying loop active — rather than settling into a defensive stance. Closing out, Ambitious's overkill redirection can clear multiple blockers off a single attack to open a battlefield for conquest, while Mercenary's Ganking lets it relocate to whichever battlefield is weakest after a big-spending turn.

Win condition: winning Showdowns outright through combat efficiency and mobile pressure; the Gold gear token trickle from rune recycling is a minor resource supplement to that aggression, not an independent holding or grind plan.
