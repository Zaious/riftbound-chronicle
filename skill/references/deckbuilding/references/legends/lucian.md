### Lucian - Purifier (Fury+Body)

Legend ability: `Your Equipment each give Assault. (+1 might while equipped unit is an attacker.)` **Lucian - Gunslinger** (Fury): `Assault. When I attack, deal damage equal to my Assault to an enemy unit here.` **Lucian - Merciless** (Body): `Weaponmaster. The first time I conquer each turn, ready me.`

**Archetype**: Aggro. The Legend ability is a static, always-on buff to every Equipment card, but it only matters while attacking (Assault applies to attackers only), so the whole plan structurally points toward being the attacker rather than the defender, and needs a real Equipment sub-package to be worth anything at all. Gunslinger converts that same Assault bonus directly into removal — an immediate, attack-triggered damage effect that doubles the value of a single piece of Equipment (bigger attacker and a removal ping in one action). Merciless is a tempo piece: her on-play Equip discount gets a weapon attached the turn she lands, and "the first time I conquer each turn, ready me" lets her act again immediately after a successful conquest.

**Core unfolding**: Early game wants Equipment attached and attacking fast — Merciless's discounted Weaponmaster gets an armed attacker down the same turn she's played. Once running, Gunslinger turns every attack into both bigger combat stats and a free damage ping against whatever's opposing it, clearing blockers and pushing through in the same motion, while Merciless's conquer-then-ready extends a single turn's tempo by letting a successful attacker immediately follow up or reposition to defend newly taken ground. Nothing here scales explosively — no cost-reduction snowball, no delayed payoff — it's a steady per-turn Equipment-fueled attack plan.

**Engine to points**: A win-the-Showdown-by-attacking plan — Assault only functions on offense, Gunslinger's damage clears the way to conquest, and Merciless's extra readying exists to keep pressing rather than to generate cards. Nothing in this Legend's kit produces draw or independent resource advantage; the whole engine converts Equipment investment straight into successful attacks.
