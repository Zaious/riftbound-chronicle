### Vex, Gloomist (Calm+Chaos)

> Tier 2-confirmed NOT CONFIRMED 2026-08-18 (`references/verification-log.md`) — the hold-oriented control identity is solidly real (riftbound.gg: "Vex is playing a hold oriented, control play style, designed to get set on a location and control whatever unit the opponent might develop to contest it"), but neither analyzed Champion is the real default. A third print this entry never considers, **Vex, Apathetic** (Unleashed set, UNL-150, Chaos domain, Deflect + Stun-on-opponent-unit-play), is riftbound.gg's explicit "key unit" and CORE at 82.73% presence per riftDecks.com — well ahead of Mocking (GEM, 6.36%) and Cheerless (FLEX, 10.91%), both of which are real, above-average performers when included but nowhere near the deck's actual default. riftbound.gg also states the deck's late-game win condition is itself unsettled in current competitive play, not a fixed loop.

Legend ability: `When you or an ally hold, you may exhaust me to draw 1.` **Vex, Mocking** (Calm): `Shield. Tank. When you Stun an enemy unit at a battlefield, you may move me to that battlefield.` **Vex, Cheerless** (Chaos): `While I'm in combat, friendly spells cost 1 Energy and a rainbow rune less to a minimum of 1 Energy, and enemy spells cost 1 Energy and a rainbow rune more.`

The Legend ability's trigger is "hold" — the literal scoring action of the win condition — converted directly into card draw. That's an unambiguous control signal, and real coverage backs it up almost verbatim: riftbound.gg frames the deck as built "to get set on a location and control whatever unit the opponent might develop to contest it." The ability doesn't reward attacking or conquering, it rewards the specific act of sitting on a Battlefield you already have, refueling your hand precisely for having done so. **In real decks, the Champion doing the early legwork for that plan is neither Mocking nor Cheerless — it's Vex, Apathetic**, a Chaos-domain print from the Unleashed set that isn't part of either half of this entry's analysis at all: "Vex - Apathetic is the key unit[s] the legend wants to develop early, and keep as safe as possible" (riftbound.gg), CORE at 82.73% presence (riftDecks.com), splashed heavily into other Legends' decks too (Irelia, Diana, Draven, Ezreal) as a generic Deflect/Stun disruption piece. Mocking reinforces the control read with stats built to survive combat as a defender (Shield, Tank) plus a reactive relocation trigger off Stunning an enemy, and Cheerless is a Showdown-combat tax; both are real and outperform when included (GEM 6.36%/1.83x for Mocking, FLEX 10.91%/2.00x for Cheerless) — genuine hidden-gem-tier includes, not traps — but real decks build around them far less often than around Apathetic.

Core unfolding: get a Vex body established on a Battlefield early enough to start holding, then let the Legend ability's draw trigger fire every turn that hold persists — this is a slow-building card-advantage engine that only works once you already have board position, so the early game is about securing that first foothold cheaply rather than overextending, and real decks do this with Apathetic rather than either analyzed Champion. Once running, Mocking's Stun-triggered movement and Cheerless's combat tax are both real (if secondary) tools inside that same hold shell. Where the text-only read overreaches is in describing a settled closing loop: riftbound.gg states plainly that as of its most recent update, "the win condition past getting set on a battlefield and keeping the opponent in check is still up in the air. Some Vex decks are building around the Hidden synergy, other run high-cost units for a strong late game" — the community itself hasn't converged on how this deck is supposed to finish, a real uncertainty a kit-only derivation has no way to surface. Competitively, Vex has cooled since an early hot streak (two Top-cuts in its first three Unleashed regionals) into a currently middling Legend, Tier 4 and trending down on the current meta tier list.
