### Sivir - Battle Mistress (Body+Chaos)

> Tier 2-confirmed NOT CONFIRMED 2026-08-18 (`references/verification-log.md`) — real coverage labels this Midrange/ramp, not aggro, and the actual named win condition is entirely outside Sivir's own kit: "Sivir's most played and best deck is one built around Dazzling Aurora, capitalizing on the power of... Elder Dragon" (riftbound.gg), corroborated by a real 2026-08-08 tournament list (hextechanalytics.com) running that exact ramp package. Sivir, Mercenary is the real Chosen Champion (matching this entry), but Sivir, Ambitious is so unplayed she doesn't even register in riftDecks.com's 78 tracked cards for this Legend — more extreme than "fringe," effectively zero real presence.

Legend ability: `When you recycle a rune, you may exhaust me to play a Gold gear token exhausted. When one or more enemy units die, ready me.` **Sivir - Ambitious** (Body): `Deflect 2. When I conquer after an attack, if you assigned 5 or more excess damage to enemy units, you may deal that much to an enemy unit.` **Sivir - Mercenary** (Chaos): `Accelerate. If you've spent at least two rainbow runes this turn, I have +2 might and Ganking.`

Archetype: aggro is the text-only read, but real coverage describes something closer to midrange/ramp instead. hextechanalytics.com calls it a deck for "Midrange players who enjoy planning the resource turn before the scoring turn," and riftbound.gg describes the actual competitive shell as one that spends its early turns "slowing down the opponent" and "disrupting them later on" while ramping toward a big off-kit payoff — the opposite of a race. The Legend does have two reactive triggers — rune recycling and enemy units dying — but real decks use them to bank a Gold/Power resource curve toward that ramp target, not to fuel an aggressive race. **Sivir, Mercenary is confirmed the real default Champion** (CORE, 75.61% presence per riftDecks.com; "Chosen Champion" per hextechanalytics.com, run CHOSEN in a real 17th-place Riftbound Showdown Ottawa list, 2026-08-08). **Sivir, Ambitious's excess-damage redirect is not part of real decks at all** — she is absent from riftDecks.com's entire 78-card tracked list for this Legend, a more total absence than any other "unplayed second Champion" case checked so far.

Core unfolding: the text-only read is that Sivir wants to attack early to rack up excess damage and kills, feeding both Champions' combat-forward payoffs. **In real play, the actual engine looks different**: recycle a rune for an exhausted Gold token, use an enemy death to ready Sivir for another, and spend the accumulated Gold/Power to establish **Dazzling Aurora**, which reveals cards until it finds a unit and plays it for free — most commonly **Elder Dragon**, both CORE cards (63.41% presence each per riftDecks.com) entirely outside Sivir's own printed kit. A real Shadow's Pick tournament list (hextechanalytics.com, 2026-08-08) runs exactly this: Catalyst of Aeons and Mobilize to build a rune base, Dazzling Aurora and Last Rites to find and rebuy Elder Dragon, Gust/Flurry of Blades for early disruption, and only a single copy of Sivir, Mercenary as the sole Champion — no Ambitious at all. A separate, entirely different real archetype ("Miracle Sivir," per Cardsrealm) instead ramps Power efficiency by filling the trash pile to discount **Rhasa the Sunderer**, another off-kit bomb — a second real Sivir build this entry's text-only derivation has no way to see, since neither payoff is printed on Sivir or either of her Champions.

Win condition: the text-only read is winning Showdowns outright through combat efficiency and mobile pressure, with the Gold gear trickle as a minor supplement. Real coverage names the actual win condition explicitly and differently: "we already mentioned the win condition, based on Dazzling Aurora summoning Elder Dragon" (riftbound.gg) — a ramp-into-a-single-huge-threat plan, backed by disruption (Mindsplitter, Gust) to protect it, not a combat-stats race.
